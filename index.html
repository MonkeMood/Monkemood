Add your own costume.

Every sticker here is just an image file in the repo’s stickers/ folder. Drop a new one in, push, and it shows up on this page.

  1. Fork the repo (or just edit it, if it’s yours).
  2. Add your image to stickers/<category>/your-sticker.webp (transparent PNG/WebP, about 440 px).
  3. Push or open a PR. The manifest rebuilds itself. Done.
